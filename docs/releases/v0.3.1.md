## v0.3.1 · Silent recording, higher recording level

This release changes two things.

1. **Recording is silent from start to end**. The user decided this: recording is silent, for personal use. The device is only for its owner, so all cue sounds are gone and the screen is the only cue.
2. **The recording level is higher** (see "Mic gain" below). The user speaks at a normal speaking volume while holding the device.

### Changes
- **Sounds that no longer play**: memo started and saved, meeting started and saved, too short or cancelled, refused or failed. The device makes no sound while recording or when it refuses to record; the speaker is never turned on.
- **Screen cues**: the `● REC` and level bar on the recording page, `●12:31` in the status bar, and the existing refusal reasons (for example "Recording needs an SD card") all stay.
- **Memo prompt**: while getting ready, the prompt on screen changes from "Speak after the beep" to "**Speak when you see ● REC**".
- **Meeting confirmation unchanged**: after `Opt+R` the "Tell the people present first" dialog still appears, and recording starts only after Enter; ending also needs a confirmation.
- With no SD card (or a nosd build) the audio task no longer starts: it was only used to play the refusal sound and now has nothing to do. This saves about 6 KB of RAM.
- The firmware reports version `cpa-fw/0.3.1`.

### Mic gain
- **The old problem**: in two recordings where the user held the device and spoke at normal volume (7.4 s, 57.3 s), the sample peak was only −23.7 dBFS and the RMS of the voiced parts was about −45 dBFS. All gain was applied after the 16-bit samples (M5Unified `magnification` 16, `over_sampling` 1, which is ×8), so every sample was a multiple of 8 and the voice used only about ±267 quantization levels.
- **The new approach**: the gain moves into the ES8311 codec and is applied before the samples become 16-bit.
  - After every `Mic.begin()`, write the PGA (REG14) and the ADC volume (REG17), then read them back to check. `Mic.begin()` writes both registers back to the M5Unified defaults every time, so they are rewritten at every recording start.
  - At most 3 tries per register. If it is still wrong, recording goes on as usual: that register stays at the M5 default, the level will be lower than expected, but the recording is not lost.
  - `magnification` becomes 2. With `over_sampling` fixed at 1, 2 means no amplification, and the samples are kept as they are.
  - Memos and meetings use the same gain.
- **Target**: holding the device and speaking at normal volume, the sample peak of the whole recording is around −12 dBFS, with −14 to −10 allowed.
- **Chosen values** (HIL `mic_cal` calibration, 2026-10-21: the Mac's built-in speaker played reference tones and Chinese and English read-aloud speech, and the device recorded 11 gain steps):
  - PGA: 9 (+27 dB). REG17: 0xC4 (+2.5 dB). `magnification`: 2 (no amplification). A total of +29.5 dB in the codec, about 11.4 dB higher than the v0.3.0 level.
  - Expected peak for this user: −11.9 dBFS. RMS of voiced parts: −35.2 dBFS. Noise floor in silence: −73.7 dBFS (median of 100 ms windows after a 150 Hz high-pass; about −68 dBFS unfiltered).
  - `lead_ms`: still 150. With the new gain, 5 silent starts showed a start-up transient of 70–90 ms.
  - Why this step: the 5 ways of splitting +30 dB (PGA 6–10, the rest in REG17) all had an SNR between 37.4 and 39.0 dB, so the rule picked PGA 10. That gave an expected peak of −11.4 dBFS; fine-tuning half a step toward the −12 target, the rule landed on PGA 9 plus 2.5 dB of ADC volume. Both steps sound the same.
- **The test firmware adds `debug.mic{pga 0–10, vol 0–255, mag 1–64}`**, which replies `ack`.
  - The gain set takes effect from the next recording start; the segment being recorded is not affected. For calibration only.
  - The release firmware does not know it and replies `E_UNKNOWN_TYPE`.
  - Protocol v0 only adds things; `v` stays 0.
- **The Mac side should follow**: WhisperWoof's silence threshold `CARDPUTER_SILENCE_RMS` should go up from 0.0005 to **0.00084** (the value from calibration: the dB midpoint between the noise floor and the estimated level of "speaking 1 m from the device"). The room measured quieter than expected: the unfiltered noise floor is about 0.0004, 0.0005 is only a little above it, and segments with nobody speaking are easily taken as voiced. cpad does not need to change.

### Unchanged
- Gestures (hold G0 to record a memo, `Opt+R` → Enter to record a meeting), the 400 ms anti-mistrigger delay, refusal rules and the reasons on screen, the 5-minute and 3-hour limits, ★ marks.
- Recording contract v1, protocol v0 (only `debug.mic` was added in the test firmware), segmentation, `file.*` transfer. cpad does not need to change; WhisperWoof only adjusts its silence threshold (see above).

### Known issues
- **The `lead_ms` margin depends on the digital gain**: with the new gain the silent-start transient is 70–90 ms, and 150 ms is enough. But the transient comes after the PGA and grows with REG17 and `magnification`: at v0.3.0's ×8 the silent-start transient lasted 260 ms. If the digital gain goes up again later, retest `lead_ms` per docs/gates/m3-device-gates.md §5.2.
- **In the first recording after power-on, the first 0.75–1 s is all zeros** (#31, present since v0.3.0): both of the user's memos were the first recording after power-on, and their first 754 ms and 994 ms of samples were all zeros; the meeting recorded right after did not have this. The recording engine does not pad zeros; the zeros come from the mic path, most likely the analog warm-up of the ES8311 after power-up (M5Unified PR #348 recorded about 1 s on the same codec). HIL did not catch it because the device was always on USB and the codec always powered. The first 512 ms of zeros in that `test_mic_gain` run fit this explanation too. The result is that the first second or so of the first memo after power-on is lost. Fix in v0.3.2: warm up the codec once after power-on; and show ● REC and start writing the file only after the first non-zero block of sound arrives.
- **Meeting level not measured separately**: the gain was set for handheld memos and is shared by memos and meetings. Whether a real meeting recorded 1–2 m from the device falls below WhisperWoof's threshold still needs a real meeting to check.
- **Speaking volume measured only twice**: on v0.3.1 the user recorded one more handheld memo: peak −16.5 dBFS (v0.3.0: −23.7), RMS of voiced parts −38.2 dBFS (v0.3.0: −46.7), 3,517 distinct sample values with a greatest common divisor of 1 (v0.3.0: 361 and 8). Resolution is fully restored and the level is about 8 dB higher, but 4.5 dB below the −12 prediction. With only one memo, it is not clear whether the user spoke softly or held it farther away this time; later recordings will tell.
- The other open items listed for v0.3.0 stay as they were (#21, #22, the items of #23 other than cue volume, #14). The listening check of cue volume is no longer needed.
