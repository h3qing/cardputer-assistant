## v0.5.1 · Recordings wait for the right Mac; English first, Chinese supported

This release answers one requirement: a recording meant for another Mac must not be lost just because this Mac was plugged in first. The device now keeps a copy of what it delivered, can deliver it again to another Mac, and asks for a moment when you plug in with recordings made offline. It also makes the docs, `cpa`, the installer and the Mac app English first, with Chinese supported, and fixes a race in the installer's service step.

The device, cpad and the Mac app ship together. Protocol v0 does not change; kept copies live on the device only, so old and new firmware and old and new cpad mix freely. WhisperWoof keeps working.

### Device: keep memos until the right Mac (docs/28 S1a, #84)
- **Kept copies**: when a Mac pulls a recording, the device no longer deletes it. It writes one line to a delivery ledger on the SD card (`SENT.L`: which recording went to which Mac, as a 4-byte hash) and keeps the files for 7 days. Kept copies are invisible to every Mac and take none of the 256 waiting slots. The oldest go first when the card has less than 1 GB free or more segments than the boot scan can hold.
- **Deliver again**: Settings → Storage (Opt+M, then `,`) → "Send again". It shows which Mac got the last batch ("from 3F2A: 2"); the confirm box opens on Cancel, `/` Enter confirms. That batch is waiting again, and **the Mac that took it never sees it again**: the next other Mac you plug into takes it.
- **Give to this Mac**: the second row of Settings → Storage undoes "deliver again" (and the hint's "no", below), so the Mac that is connected gets what was held back from it.
- **The plug-in hint**: when you plug into a Mac with recordings made since the cable was last pulled, the hint bar says "2 offline go to this Mac · Tab: no" for 5 seconds. Press nothing and they go as usual; Tab then Enter keeps them from this Mac. While the device is locked or the Tab menu is open you cannot see the hint, so the 5 seconds do not start and those recordings stay held, for at most 2 minutes.
- **Clear kept copies**: Settings → Storage → "Clear kept", confirm box on Cancel. The lock card and the Settings sync row show "kept N" while there are any. **Kept copies are plain text on the card** (SECURITY.md): before you take the device somewhere you do not trust, sync to zero and clear them.
- Switching Macs quickly works: if you unplug from one Mac and plug into another straight away, the new Mac now pulls at once (before the fix found on the device it waited 60 s).
- The firmware reports `cpa-fw/0.5.1`.

### English first, with Chinese supported (#88–#92)
- **Docs**: English is at the normal path and Chinese next to it as `*.zh-CN.md`, with a language switch on line 1 (CONTRIBUTING). This release also translates CHANGELOG, SECURITY, docs/07, docs/28 and `firmware/sim/README.md`.
- **`cpa`, cpad and cpa-stt** speak English unless the language says Chinese: `CPA_LANG`, then `cpa lang zh|en|auto` (`lang` in `config.json`), then the Mac's locale. `cpa filing triage`, `cpa dest test --send` and `cpa setup check` used to be Chinese only, or Chinese and English side by side; now they use one language. Daily-note lines, meeting titles and destination labels are written in the language of the moment; lines already written are never rewritten. `cpa service install` passes the locale to the background service.
- **Installer**: `install.sh` and `uninstall.sh` print one language, English by default; `--lang zh` (or `CPA_LANG=zh`, or a Chinese locale) gives Chinese. The final checklist follows it.
- **Mac app**: screenshots are drawn in English into `out/shots/` and in Chinese into `out/shots/zh/`, with English demo data. The app itself already had both languages.
- **HIL tools**: comments in English; a run whose start language cannot be read leaves the device in English.

### Other
- **Installer: the background service no longer races the old one** (#83). `cpa service install` waits for the old cpad to stop, then checks that the new one is really running, retries once, and otherwise stops with a clear message; `install.sh` trusts only that answer.
- **Design: idle sleep** (docs/29, #86): light sleep when the screen is dark on battery, deep sleep after 2 hours, shutdown on low battery. Design only; not in this firmware.

### Upgrade
- **On the Mac**: run the installer one-liner again (README "Install"); running it again upgrades:

  ```bash
  curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/h3qing/cardputer-assistant/main/scripts/install.sh | bash
  ```

  Add `bash -s -- --lang zh` for Chinese output.
- **The device**: flash `cpa-v0.5.1.bin` from this release (`… | bash -s -- --firmware` also checks it and puts it in `~/Downloads`). On the main Mac: sync first (`↑0` and R0), `cpa pause 120`, then write it to 0x0 as in README "Install method A". Writing the merged image erases NVS, so settings such as the language go back to their defaults; recordings and kept copies on the SD card are not touched.

### Known issues
- **A task-watchdog reboot can happen during "deliver again" or "Clear kept"** with about 100 segments waiting and 200 kept. It is data-safe: the device restarts and nothing is lost. If it happens, sync first so fewer are waiting, then try again. #85 fixes it.
- **Kept copies are plain text** until SEAL encryption (docs/26) exists; clear them before going somewhere you do not trust (SECURITY.md).
- **GitHub Actions is suspended**: all the checks above ran locally. Run them again on GitHub once Actions is back.
- **WhisperWoof does not yet recognise `cpa-stt`'s claim file** (docs/22 §4.10): only one of them can be on.
- The real-device gates listed in v0.4.1 (carry it for a day, run a real meeting at your own pace, pull and reinsert the SD card) still need you.
