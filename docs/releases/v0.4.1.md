## v0.4.1 · The steady release: the Mando theme, and a wrong press on the small screen is now visible

This release answers the 10-22 requirement: this is a small device with a small screen and keyboard, so convenience and stability come first. The interaction review (docs/20) found that the v0.4.0 base is solid; the problem is "you don't know you pressed the wrong thing": the device has no sound and no vibration, the screen is the only feedback, yet many places show nothing. This release finishes every fix from the review, and ships them together with the Mando theme (docs/19) and the issues found in the v0.4.0 long real-device test. The three requests from the 10-22 voice memos are in it too (next section).

All device changes are in the firmware. cpad changed a few small things to match (session names, sid de-duplication, `uncertain` in the queue and `cpa queue reopen`, project tags after meeting segment 0; see the sections below). The same tag also carries three Mac-side items from main: `cpa-stt`, `cpa rec ls` showing seconds, and cpad installed from a wheel (see "On the Mac"). Protocol v0 is additive only: old and new firmware and old and new cpad can be mixed freely. WhisperWoof still works, but it does not yet recognise the claim file that `cpa-stt` brings, so only one of the two can run (see Known issues). The device UI of the originally planned v0.4.1 "recording history and to-dos" moves to v0.4.2 (D10, docs/13 §15).

### Three requests from voice memos (gh #51, docs/20 U26)
1. **Memos no longer cut off mid-sentence**: requirement: a memo held on G0 must not stop on its own while you are still talking. The cause: a single bad-contact sample of the side key was taken as a release (M5Unified's debounce does not work when one loop pass takes longer than 10 ms). Now a G0 release only counts when two samples at least 30 ms apart both read it. After release there is also a 0.6-second grace period; the page says "Let go · hold within 0.6 s". Pressing back during it continues the same segment and the same file; only if you don't press back does it end and save. This is the same whether you hold to record over the editor, a confirm box or a menu (D4), or on the lock card. The press-back belongs only to this recording: it does not submit a draft or open Note.
2. **Double-tap G0 for hands-free recording; press once more to end**: requirement: holding is tiring, so a double tap of G0 should start a voice mode and the next press should close it. Wherever a single tap of G0 means "Note" (home, every page; when the Tab menu is open, the first tap closes the menu first), two short presses start recording the moment the second press goes down (no 0.5-second hold detection wait; still speak once you see ● REC), and it keeps going after release. The page says "Hands-free · tap G0 to end". Pressing G0 once more ends and saves; a double tap also ends it and does not then open Note. While recording, 1–9 / 0 set the project, Tab opens the menu, Opt+L locks the screen without stopping the recording; other keys do nothing (an accidental press in a pocket does not end it). The 5-minute limit still applies; the last 30 seconds show "0:30 left". When locked, it only counts on a card lit by a key press (on a black screen, or when the card lit itself, the first press only lights the card). When locked during hands-free recording, G0 on a black screen only lights the card (it says "Hands-free: [G0]ends it"); one more press ends it, and the card says "√ Saved". A slightly slow second press (within 1 second, no other key between) still counts; tap then hold is still hold-to-talk. Note after a single tap now opens only after 0.35 seconds (to see whether a second press comes); if you type during that time, the first character goes straight in.
3. **The waveform follows your voice, not the room hum**: in four sample memos, speech blocks were 7–19 dB quieter than first assumed, so on a linear scale they were all drawn as a single dot. The level is now drawn in dB (-60 dBFS is a dot, -24 dBFS is full), and speech fills 5–9 cells. The level path first goes through a 4th-order 130 Hz high-pass, so room hum at 35–68 Hz drops by 22–46 dB, pauses show dots, and Mando no longer nods along to the hum. The recorded WAV is unchanged, byte for byte.

### Decisions (docs/20 §3; on 10-22 all of D1–D10 were set to the recommended defaults)
1. **D1 Single letters on home**: on home, R and L need Opt (Opt+R starts a meeting, Opt+L locks); the other cells are still single keys. Pressing a letter that is not a cell shows how to take a Note, once per visit to home.
2. **D2 Auto-lock**: on battery, the device locks as soon as the screen goes off (60 seconds with no key press); plugged into the Mac it does not auto-lock. The cost: taking a typed Note on a black screen takes three presses, Opt+L, Enter, G0. A spoken memo is still "tap, then hold".
3. **D3 The two meeting boxes**: on the start box, Opt+L cancels and locks; 15 seconds with no press cancels; typing or a digit also cancels. On the end box, Opt+L keeps recording and locks; 10 seconds with no press returns to the meeting page. Both boxes count from the last key press and do not time out while the Tab menu is open. The start box does not count as recording (the screen can go off, and the Mac can pull recordings). On the meeting page, ` only hints "In a meeting: Opt+R ends it". An Enter within 400 ms that is too fast shows "Too fast: press Enter again" instead of being silently swallowed. **The meeting confirm box wording is unchanged**.
4. **D4 Hold G0 over an overlay**: when not recording, you can hold G0 to record a memo even with the editor, a confirm box or a menu open; the draft is kept.
5. **D5 Back key in the editor**: in the editor, ` exits (asks "Discard?" first if there is text); the backtick character is now typed with Fn+`. No parked drafts.
6. **D6 ` on confirm boxes**: on the new-session and reply confirm boxes, ` returns to the editor, keeping the text and the target.
7. **D7 When the card lights while locked**: for 2 seconds at the moment of locking; for 2 seconds when a recording ends (including auto-stop), showing the result; for 3 seconds when the "needs you" count goes up while plugged into the Mac, showing only the number. Enter or Space while locked does not light it.
8. **D8 Start of a memo**: no pre-roll. As soon as G0 goes down (and this press will record), it shows "● Wait"; still "Speak once you see ● REC".
9. **D9 Key combos while a menu is open**: pressing a combo written on the menu (for example Opt+L) closes the menu and then runs it.
10. **D10 Release**: these ship with the theme as v0.4.1; the device UI for recording history and to-dos becomes v0.4.2.

### New features
- **Mando companion theme** (docs/19, #45):
  - WhisperWoof's warm brown palette (the canvas is still 8-bit, now with an indexed palette), rounded cards and keycaps. The home grid, project page, needs-you list, lock card, recording page, Tab menu and status bar are all redrawn.
  - Mando has eight looks: here, listening, sniffing, needs you, a hop when saved, didn't hear, napping, offline. Timers and the clock use large Nunito digits.
  - It only animates at full brightness: 200 ms beat, partial refresh, a breathing recording ●, a scrolling waveform, a meeting timeline with ★, the Tab menu sliding in from the right. Display SPI time while recording drops from about 104 to 21–30 ms/s.
  - `lib/theme` is the public drawing API for modules and third-party apps; copyright for images and fonts is in NOTICE.
- **Lock card** (docs/20 B1):
  - When a G0 hold is swallowed while locked, the space where the clock was shows large text "Not recording / Let go, tap, then hold", and Mando tilts its head instead of napping (napping would read as "all fine"). The text stays until the card goes off or another key is pressed.
  - When a recording ends while locked, the card shows the result: "√ Saved 0:12", "× Too short", the reason for an auto-stop ("Meeting: low battery", "5 min limit reached"), or why it did not record. Only status and numbers, no names.
  - Badges: Waiting N (not drawn without data; data left from before the Mac disconnected shows "Locked · offline" at the top of the card, and the badge is not yellow), RN and ↑N (not drawn when 0), or "√ synced". Battery is yellow below 30% and red below 17%.
- **Recording page** (B2): a memo shows "0:30 left" from 4:30; the meeting page keeps "Bat N%"; below 30% the start box says "Battery: about N min"; the meeting hint bar again says "Space adds ★".
- **Status bar** (B3): has a width budget and never overlaps; zero values are not drawn; ※k is lit for only 10 seconds; ※SD means recording is supported but there is no usable SD card.
- **Home** (B4): when offline, the greeting says "offline now" and the most urgent row says "Offline · as of N min ago", and Mando switches to its offline look; the most urgent row lists needs-you items first, then the most recently idle; items idle for more than a day are collapsed; when recording is not possible the R cell is grey and the G0 hint says why.
- **Inbox and board** (B5): the hint bar says, for the selected row, whether Enter can deliver; full names, and the confirm page shows the end of the name plus a short id; a new session with an unknown result is marked "open?" on the board.
- **Wording and layout** (B6): the confirm box card and the hint bar use the same verb ("[Enter]start [`]edit"), and Tab menu rows follow the box text; the editor wraps at spaces; every fixed hint fits in the theme's hint-bar pill (224 px).
- **A hint for a key that does nothing** (U17): Opt+H/I/P/M with the editor open, position keys or Enter on the meeting page, the description row in a menu, the selected row in a list that is gone; the same hint is not repeated within 3 seconds.
- `debug.state.diag` in the test firmware (#21): free heap and its low-water mark, largest block, stack headroom for the main loop and audio task, loop-time counters, keyboard counters; after a recording, `debug.state.rec` carries health counters. Protocol v0 additive only (protocol/README item 21); release firmware does not have it.
- `cpa queue reopen <qid>`: undo on the Mac a row hit with Opt+D by mistake on the device (U12).
- Protocol v0 additive only: optional field `uncertain` on each item of `queue.snapshot` (protocol/README item 22). When a session is opened from such an item, the confirm box adds a line "May have started: see Sessions" (U25).
- Runtime fuzz testing in CI (`test_fuzz_runtime`, about 3 seconds per run); `fuzz-nightly.yml` runs the full matrix every night.
- Firmware version reports `cpa-fw/0.4.1`.

### Changes
- **Hint bar** (U20): the result of your own action shows at once, replacing a queued "X needs you"; errors show for 4.5 seconds; while locked or with the screen off, hints do not expire, and after the screen lights only the latest recording result and error remain.
- **Every recording end says how it ended and how long it was** (U6): "Meeting: low battery · saved 42:10", "5 min limit reached · saved 5:00". When a recording stops by itself while unlocked, the screen lights as if a key were pressed so the result is visible, then dims, goes off and locks as usual.
- If G0 is still held when a memo reaches the 5-minute limit, the screen stays on "5 min reached: saved / Let go of [G0]" until release; it does not go off even on battery (U15).
- Confirm boxes and their hint bars fit on one line and no longer mention "v0.4"; a session that needs you is no longer described as "running" (U7).
- Consistent wording (U22): Note · AI queue · New session · Reply; ages in words ("2 h", and "45min" on the right of list rows); terminal / background instead of tty / bg.
- On battery, the screen dims for 10 seconds before going off (U24). When a G0 hold lights the lock card, it stays lit at most 10 seconds longer than usual (a side key pressed in a pocket does not keep it lit and drain the battery).
- Enter on "Discard draft?" has 400 ms of accidental-press protection, and the first row of the Tab menu is "Back to editing" (U18).
- Slots without a project (U13): queuing or opening a session to a project that does not exist hints "Opt+digit: 1 acmeshop · 2 cardputer", keeps the draft, and no longer reports E_STORAGE.
- SD card error during use (U16): the hint says "SD card error: reseat it, restart"; with internal storage, captures in the meantime go to the internal cpafs first.
- Lists remember the selected row by sid / qid, so after a refresh Enter / Opt+D does not land on another row (U12).
- cpad: when sending session names, it strips its own project's `key/` prefix; a task dictated in Chinese is named from its first 8 characters (U14).

### Fixes
- **Meeting box silently swallowed Enter and did not go away** (U1): see D3. Before, with a quick "Opt+R, Enter, Opt+L, pocket", the box stayed, and a later Enter in the pocket started a meeting recording.
- **The recording close event was lost, the device stuck in closing, only a restart helped** (S1): stop is sent again after 5 seconds; after 10 seconds it returns to idle and hints "Didn't close: parts are on the card". Each recording carries a code, and a late end event only matches its own recording.
- A project tag set or cleared after meeting segment 0 now uses the latest value on the Mac (#46).
- cpad de-duplicates by 8-character sid: one duplicate sid no longer makes the device reject the whole `sessions.snapshot`.
- Tests: the snapshot strings in `test_runtime_budget` reach the protocol limit, with the budget based on real Chinese data sizes; HIL's `Device.home()` waits out the 400 ms protection before pressing Enter; HIL's `latency` closes the empty editor with ` (after D5, Fn+` types a backtick).
- The review after D1–D10 found 9 more issues, all fixed (docs/20 §5.6): the lock card flipped back to an old "√ Saved" after a swallowed hold was released; a confirm box's menu said "Enter confirms"; the lock card said "Waiting 0" without data; "Answer the box first" stayed after the box was gone; HIL `latency` could not close the editor; the end box timed out under the menu and the next Enter fell through; a swallowed hold kept the card lit; a meeting that stopped itself while unlocked showed no result; "● Wait" showed even when the press would be rejected.

### On the Mac (carried by the same tag, from main)
- **`cpa-stt`** (docs/22 §4, VC-1, #52): a standalone program that transcribes recordings on this Mac. Like WhisperWoof, it is a transcriber under recording contract v1. The contract says only one may write at a time (claim file `recordings/.transcriber`); WhisperWoof does not follow this yet, so only one of the two can run (see Known issues). cpad contains no transcription code. **Off by default**: `cpa-stt setup` looks for existing ggml models (links them only after asking you; no copy, no download), `cpa-stt use` turns it on, `cpa-stt run --once` / `watch` runs it, and only `cpa-stt service install` installs the LaunchAgent. Commands are wrapped in `sandbox-exec` (no network); folders that have a `result.json` are not touched; a broken environment only writes `blocked`, and `cpa-stt retry` after fixing it. `cpa status` gains a "transcriber" line.
- `cpa rec ls` and `cpa rec show`: durations under 10 minutes show seconds (`51s`, `4m59s`), so short memos no longer all show `1m` (#55).
- cpad installed with `uv tool install ./daemon` no longer reports a missing `limits.json` on every command (#57): `limits.json` ships inside the wheel; CI builds the wheel and runs it once in a fresh venv outside the repo.

### Unchanged
- Other global keys: G0 Note (it just opens 0.35 seconds later), Opt+1–9, Opt+H/P/I/M, Opt+N/Q/S, Opt+R, Tab, Opt+L; unlocking is still Opt+L then Enter within 3 seconds.
- The meeting confirm box wording ("Start a meeting recording? / Tell everyone here first"), `consent:"ack"`, the 5-minute and 3-hour limits, ★ marks, silence throughout recording.
- The lock screen is not a security lock (SECURITY.md); the lock card only shows numbers, never a project, session name or body text.
- Protocol v0 additive only; `file.*` transfer unchanged; recording contract v1 additive only (the claim file `recordings/.transcriber` from `cpa-stt`, `by` and `reason: engine` in `status.json`, `cap` in `manifest.json`); WhisperWoof still works, but running it alongside `cpa-stt` has to wait until it recognises `.transcriber` (see Known issues).

### Known issues
- **Double taps have not been counted on the real device** (docs/07 §8.3 U26-1): double-tap 20 times each on home, on the board, and right after taking a Note, and record how many were not recognised. Debouncing makes a G0 release count at least 30 ms later (later when the loop is slow; the longest pass in the long test was 108 ms), so a press close to 0.5 seconds may become a hold, and with a very slow loop two presses close together may merge into one. A missed double tap should never record by mistake: at most it opens Note, or gives a "Too short: discarded" recording.
- **Note opens 0.35 seconds later**: it waits for a second press. The HIL capture page latency limit was relaxed from 300 ms to 650 ms (docs/04 AC1).
- **The waveform high-pass cost is an estimate**: about 0.6–0.9 ms per block (ESP32-S3, estimated from disassembly, not measured on the device).
- **The real-device gates that need a person have not run**: UX-H1 (carry it for a day, including auto-lock on battery and the memo gesture on the lock screen), UX-H2 running a real meeting at your own speed, UX-H3 (unplugging and reinserting the SD card). Done by script on the real device: the H2 key timing, H5, auto-lock on simulated battery (docs/07 §8.4). docs/20 originally said H1 and H2 would run before v0.4.1 shipped; this release shipped without them, and H1 and H2 move to after release (docs/20 §5.6 updated).
- **Not run on the real device this time** (docs/07 §8.4): the nosd regression; HIL `sd_store`, `rec_cold_start` (needs the Mac to play a tone), `latency`; device tests `test_mic_gain`, `test_rec_sd`, `test_sd_absent`, `test_sd_store`, `test_sd_wake`, `test_storage` (v0.4.0 ran `test_mic_gain` among these).
- **WhisperWoof does not recognise `.transcriber` yet** (docs/22 §4.10, WhisperWoof #151): only one of the two can run; turn off WhisperWoof's Cardputer inbox before turning on `cpa-stt`. If `cpa-stt` finds someone else writing in recordings/, it stops on that folder and warns.
- **The test counter `mic_missing` has false positives** (only in `debug.state.rec` of the test firmware; release has no such counter): when a memo stops at certain moments (on the real device, 17 of 18 holds of 3,000 ms; none for one try each at 2,500, 3,032, 3,064, 3,096 ms, three at 4,000 ms, one hands-free, one meeting; also 0 across the 13 meetings in the long test), the counter goes up by 1 at the moment of stopping (already closing). From the source, it is probably the buffer re-attach failing when the microphone is closed. The recorded length is the same (3,072 ms every time), `ring_high` stays 1, and no audio is lost. The 0.6-second grace moved the stop to a different moment, which is why the integrate/v041 long test did not hit it. Later the counter will stop counting once stop has begun.
- **About the first 0.65 seconds of a memo still is not recorded** (U8): per D8, only the "● Wait" hint was added; there is no pre-roll. Speak once you see ● REC.
- **Removing and reinserting the SD card** (U16): the hint changed, but "reseat it, restart" itself is unchanged; real-device gate H3 has not run.
- Of the known issues listed for v0.4.0, "holding G0 while locked with no SD card looks like nothing happened" is fixed (the lock card says "× not recorded / Needs an SD card"); "no banner on home" still applies.
